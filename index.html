01 Research

Three connected directions

One research agenda: discover mechanisms, seek the right evidence, and adapt without losing trust.

01

Mechanistic Bayesian Reasoning

Learn explicit latent mechanisms—events, graphs, and dynamics—that support reasoning beyond prediction.

I develop Bayesian deep models that connect incomplete observations to structured latent mechanisms, combining probabilistic graphs, continuous-time dynamics, and neural representations.

Representative work
LERD · ICML 2026 STRODE · ICML 2021 Deep Graph RP · ICML 2020
02

Scientific Discovery Under Uncertainty

Build LLM and agentic systems that seek evidence, form hypotheses, and update beliefs under uncertainty.

I combine Bayesian inference with LLM reasoning and active evidence acquisition to create scientific agents that know what to observe next, when to abstain, and how to revise explanations.

Representative work
BayesAgent · AAAI 2026 BSLI · arXiv 2026 iLoRA · ICML 2026
03

Trustworthy & Safe AI for Health

Make health AI calibrated, fair, robust, and safe across populations, modalities, and distribution shifts.

I study uncertainty calibration, fairness, safe LLMs, test-time adaptation, and generative modeling so clinical and public-health AI remains dependable when evidence changes.

Representative work
TMI 2025 MICCAI 2025 LLM Guard Models · ICLR 2025
